The program in detail

What BackToHealth contains, step by step.

For the curious and for health professionals: the assessment, exercises, lessons, meditation and walking, as they are in the app.

Clinical content being validated by our doctor, also trained in physiotherapy. It may still change.

These are the lengths of the introductory programs. The app then continues, with no time limit.

At the start: a 5-minute assessment

  • 8 red-flag questions. A positive answer points to a doctor or the ER, and the program doesn’t start.
  • Pain at rest, from 0 to 10.
  • 4 movement tests (flexion, extension, right and left rotation), with the pain felt. They set the starting exercises and their level.
  • 1 to 3 activities to get back to, rated from 0 to 10 (Patient-Specific Functional Scale).
  • The rhythm: a little every day, light and active days, or a few chosen days.

Education: 23 lessons in about 4 weeks

The idea
Understanding chronic pain to regain confidence: it’s real, but it doesn’t mean the back is damaged. Then lifestyle, pain management and acceptance and commitment.
How it progresses
One 5-minute lesson per active day, in order, with a question at each step.

Exercise: 8 to 12 weeks

The idea
Moving without fear, guided by pain. The movement tests set the starting exercises: extension, flexion, rotation, trunk stabilisation, then strength (upper body, squat, lunge, hips). Up to 3/10, pain is acceptable: it doesn’t mean damage.
How it progresses
3 sets of 10 to 12 repetitions. Session at 2/10 or less: one more repetition; at 12, the next level. Above 3/10: stop, and one level down. When a family’s last level becomes easy, a harder exercise from another family is offered.
Example
Glute bridge: 3 × 10, then 3 × 11, then 3 × 12, then Bird-dog at 3 × 10.

Meditation: 8 to 11 weeks

The idea
Living with pain rather than fighting it. Secular mindfulness, practised in sessions, then useful during the day.
How it progresses
One guided session per practice, then free sessions with short reminders. 10 minutes in week 1, 15 after, up to 30 if wanted.

The main themes

  • The breath, and coming back when the mind wanders
  • The body scan, from head to feet
  • Getting to know your back
  • A more open awareness
  • Kindness: talking to yourself as to a friend
  • What matters: stopping the struggle, thoughts as clouds, your values
  • Mindful walking (optional)
  • Building your own practice

Walking: an 8-week program

The idea
Getting back into walking. A goal in minutes per week, never per day: a bad day can be made up later, or not at all.
How it progresses
It starts from what the person already walks (at least 15 minutes a week) and aims for about double in 8 weeks, from 45 to 125 minutes: about 10% more each week. Pain above 3/10: same goal. Two weeks in a row: walk as much as possible under 3/10, and start again from there.
Example
Starting at 30 minutes a week: 30, 33, 37, 40, 45, 49, 54, then 60 minutes.

After the introductory programs

  • Exercises keep adapting, with new challenges when a level becomes easy.
  • Meditation continues with the practice built, from 10 to 30 minutes.
  • Walking keeps its rhythm, or rises to about 2 hours a week.
  • Lessons stay available, and the movement tests can be redone.
  • Going further elsewhere (Pilates, yoga, meditation, clinic follow-up) is encouraged.

Follow-up

  • The chosen activities are re-rated about every 2 weeks.
  • The movement tests are repeated about every 4 weeks, to compare.
  • Pain is tracked over time, with a weekly summary.
  • During a flare-up, 3 lighter days: no exercise, comfort moments and gentle walking.
  • Data can be exported at any time.

A toolbox, not a takeover of care

BackToHealth gives the tools and the first steps. The program stands on its own; going further elsewhere (Pilates, meditation, clinic follow-up) is even better. The person stays in charge of their path.
